You are deciding between several products or plans and want the differences side by side.
You open details for each option, look for a comparison view, and note anything that is hard to compare.
You care about the full price, including fees, taxes, and anything that renews.
You do not buy or sign up. End with the option you would choose and what nearly changed your mind.

Assumptions (edit these): the service offers more than one product, plan, or tier, and their details are public.
