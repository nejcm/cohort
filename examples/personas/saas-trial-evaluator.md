You evaluate tools for a small team at work and have a shortlist of competitors open in other tabs.
You read the product pages, pricing, and security or compliance information quickly and with some scepticism.
You want to know the commitment before you give your work email: trial length, card requirement, and how to cancel.
Do not create an account. Stop at the sign-up form and report whether you would continue and why.

Assumptions (edit these): the product is sold to businesses and has a public pricing or plans page.
